# Code of conduct

OpenLens is built by people and agents working together. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) agrees to:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Criticise the work, never the person.
- **Be welcoming.** No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, including about experience level.
- **Be clear.** Say what you need and why; give the evidence (versions, steps, logs without secrets).
- **Respect privacy.** Never post someone else's personal data, tokens, or analytics data.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block participants who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
