# 🔭 OpenLens v0.5.0

**Easier to find your way, safer to keep your data, lighter on memory.** v0.5.0 groups the dashboard into eight sections with tabs and breadcrumbs, gives every feature its own page, makes every board card a door to its detail, lets you sign in with a password, and stops the cold tier from turning S3 into the only copy of your data without asking.

## ⚠️ Upgrade now if…

- **You set S3 as a second copy.** Since v0.3.0, configuring S3 also moved event days older than 120 days off the disk. From v0.5.0 that cold tier is **off until an admin turns it on**, and it refuses a bucket whose lifecycle rules expire objects in under 400 days (#417, D-032).
- **You run v0.3.x or older.** v0.5.0 fixes the Ask `k=5` anonymity bypass, the untrusted public URL, the OIDC email trust gap and the cold-tier markers lost on restart (#415). When a newer release fixes advisories, the footer now says *Security update available* (#416).

## ✨ Highlights

- 🧭 **A shorter sidebar.** Eight entries (Focus board, Ask, Live, Behaviour, Quality, People, Product, Settings), each with its own icon. A section's pages become tabs at the top, and every old URL still works (#402).
- 🍞 **Breadcrumbs everywhere, and settings that keep your place.** The instance pages (API tokens, AI, Storage, Team, My profile) keep your project's menu and share an *Instance* tab group. Time zones, quiet hours, the health path and the embed address are now pickers (#403).
- ✅ **Setup progress.** Project settings show what is left (first event, app address, an alert channel) and what is optional. Admins see a one-line reminder on the board until it is done (#403).
- 🧩 **A page per feature.** `/p/<id>/features/<key>` shows use and success against the 30 days before, routes, roles, pages, related features and findings, with Flow and History tabs (#411).
- 👉 **Every board card opens its detail**, with the board's filters carried along (#409).
- ⓘ **KPI definitions on hover.** Every tile says what it counts and over which window (#405).
- 🔑 **Password sign-in.** After the first sign-in with the admin token, *My profile* creates your admin account. Argon2id hashes, 5 failed tries lock an email for 15 minutes, and a password change signs out your other sessions. The admin token still works, folded under *Use the admin token* (#428).
- 🤖 **AI settings in two fields.** Pick Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, OpenRouter or Ollama and paste the key. OpenLens checks the key, lists the models and picks the main and fast ones. *Custom* keeps every field (#406).
- 🎞️ **Replays show active time**: "3 min active · open 2 h" instead of the time the tab stayed open (#404).
- 🧠 **Less memory.** jemalloc gives memory back after a big board. At 4 M events with four filtered boards at once, memory 30 s later drops from 1.2 GB to 135 MB (#418). Filtered boards computed together share one snapshot, and a busy project no longer invalidates the other projects' boards (#418, #420).
- 💾 **Complete backups.** `openlens backup` now holds source maps and the cold-tier markers, plus `cold-manifest.json` listing the days that live on S3 (#419).
- 🎨 **A visual pass.** Clearer titles, more space, aligned numbers and forms, visible focus rings.

## 🚀 Install or upgrade

```sh
docker run -d -p 8686:8686 -v openlens-data:/data ghcr.io/philippe-arnd/openlens:0.5
helm upgrade --install openlens oci://ghcr.io/philippe-arnd/charts/openlens -n openlens --create-namespace
```

**Coolify:** redeploy the template, or point a pinned service at `:0.5`. The first start of v0.5.0 copies the data to `backups/upgrade/`.

## ⚠️ Changes in behaviour

- **Breaking: the cold tier is opt-in** (#417, D-032). Nothing leaves the disk until an admin ticks it in *Storage and backups* or sets `OPENLENS_S3_COLD=on`. Days already on S3 stay readable. With the tier on, the S3 key needs `s3:GetLifecycleConfiguration`.
- **The login page leads with email and password** once an account has one. The admin token stays one click away. `OPENLENS_PASSWORD_LOGIN=off` keeps the old page.
- **The sidebar is grouped.** Pages moved under sections, and their URLs did not change.
- **Kubernetes readiness follows `/readyz`**, and the chart keeps `/healthz` for images before v0.3.0.

The data on disk is compatible, and nothing needs to be migrated by hand.

## 🧭 Known limitations

- **One node:** a single process with local disk.
- **Memory peak:** a cold board at 4 M events still peaks at about 1 GB, just over the chart's 1 Gi limit. Give the pod 1.5 Gi above that scale until compaction streams (#418, part 3).
- **Feature pages:** the Errors, Replays, Flags and Releases tabs come next (#411). Replays do not pause yet while the tab is idle (#404).
- **Beta:** 0.x releases may change behaviour, always announced ([docs/stability.md](../stability.md)).
